Welcome to Westbridge College. Before we begin the campus tour, I need to explain a few arrangements for your first week. Tomorrow's induction starts at ten. Your welcome email says room 207, but the group is larger than expected, so please use room 214 instead. Both rooms are on the second floor.

You may have noticed that the main library is closed. This is because of electrical repairs, rather than a shortage of staff. A temporary borrowing desk is operating in the student centre. New students can borrow up to 6 books at a time. That limit increases later in the year.

Your student card also gives you 10 pounds of printing credit. Extra credit can be purchased online. For anyone cycling to college, the new bicycle racks are behind the sports hall. The spaces beside the station belong to the railway company.

The careers team welcomes visitors without appointments this week, but you must arrive before 11 in the morning. After that, advisers will be interviewing applicants.

The shared kitchen is open all day. It is busiest between twelve and one, so eating slightly later may be more comfortable. Finally, if you cannot connect your laptop to the college network, book an online appointment with the computer support team. Taking it directly to reception will not get you technical help.
