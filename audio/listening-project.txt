Here is the final plan for our local history project. We considered analysing newspaper articles, but our tutor suggested interviews with residents because personal memories would give us a different kind of evidence. We will still read a little background material before meeting anyone.

At first, we hoped to recruit thirty people. That would take too long, so our target is now 24 participants. We want a mixture of ages, although everyone must have lived in the town for at least five years.

The tutor checked our draft questions yesterday. She thought the topics were relevant, but several questions were too technical. We need to replace specialist terms with everyday language. She did not ask us to add more questions.

Each interview should last about 15 minutes. The original idea was half an hour, but volunteers may struggle to spare that much time. We have permission to use a quiet room at the museum. A café would be convenient, but background noise would make the recordings difficult to understand.

Please finish revising the questions by Friday. Our pilot interview is on Saturday, which gives us one day to prepare. Its main purpose is to test the recording equipment, since we have not used these microphones before. After collecting all the interviews, each pair will write a summary. Keep that summary to a maximum of 600 words, excluding the list of sources.
