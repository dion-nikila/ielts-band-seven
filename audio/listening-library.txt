Welcome to the Eastfield Library. This is a short guide for new members. Basic membership is free for everyone who lives in the district. Bring a document showing your current address when you join. A passport can confirm your identity but it does not replace proof of address. The membership card lets adults borrow twelve items at once. You may have heard the old limit of eight; that changed this month.

Most books can be kept for twenty-one days. A newly released book has a shorter loan of seven days, so check the date printed on your receipt. You can renew online unless another reader has reserved the item. We no longer charge a daily late fine, but lost items still have to be replaced or paid for.

The silent study room is on the second floor. Group discussions belong in the ground-floor meeting room. You can book that room for up to two hours, and bookings open seven days ahead. Computers are on the first floor. Each computer session lasts forty-five minutes when other people are waiting. Printing is available at a small charge; scanning is free.

On weekdays, the building closes at eight in the evening. Please note that this is a change from the former closing time of six. Sunday hours are shorter, ending at four. If you arrive after closing, put returned books in the box beside the west entrance. Do not leave them at the front door. Finally, our weekly advice session for online forms takes place on Tuesday mornings. Bring the information you need, but never give staff your password.
